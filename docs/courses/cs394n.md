I am a 2nd year Computer Science Masters student. My undergraduate background is in Philosophy, Applied Math, and Computer Science. My research interests are broadly at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Computational Neuroscience. More specifically I am interested in using methods from the cognitive, computer and neural sciences to explain and model how perception and cognition are realized within humans, with the goal of improving those correlates within machines. I am currently performing research into using continuous/life-long learning paradigms with computer vision models. I am also very open to transforming this coursework into a short paper or publication if it is feasible.
